# Cardshark

Briškula, Trešeta, Bela, Poker, Rummy, L’Unico!, and Solitaire on your phone.

- **Briškula** — Triestine 40-card deck, 2–4 players, pairs, solo, three-player, or double-hand modes.
- **Trešeta** — same Italian deck, no trumps, follow suit; 4 players in pairs or 2 head to head with a stock; akuže; play to 21/31/41.
- **Bela** — mađarice (William Tell pattern), 4 players in pairs, trump calling, declarations, bela, play to 501/701/1001.
- **Poker** — Texas Hold’em and five-card draw tournaments, 2–6 players.
- **Rummy** — Croatian Remi, 2–6 players, 51-point opening, melds, lay-offs, and joker swaps.
- **L’Unico!** — colour-matching game, classic and Flip decks, 2–6 players.
- **Solitaire** — Klondike with draw-1/draw-3 and several scoring modes.

Online tables support Briškula, Trešeta, and Bela. Online Briškula supports all four offline modes; Trešeta supports 2-player head-to-head and 4-player pairs.

Single-page web app, no build step. Works offline once loaded and can be added to the Home Screen.
Card art and logo are original SVG drawn in code.
