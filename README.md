# Cardshark

Briškula and Bela on your phone, against computer players.

- **Briškula** — Triestine 40-card deck, 2 or 4 players, classic (3 cards) or double (4 cards, two plays per trick).
- **Bela** — mađarice (William Tell pattern), 4 players in pairs, trump calling, declarations, bela, play to 501/701/1001.

Single-page web app, no build step. Works offline once loaded and can be added to the Home Screen.
Card art and logo are original SVG drawn in code.
